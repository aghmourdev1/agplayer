Packed With Features

Everything you need to play anything

From local files to live streams, AG Player handles it all with a beautiful, modern interface.

Universal Format Support

Plays MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, FLV, WMV, MP3, WAV, FLAC and dozens more formats with hardware acceleration for smooth 4K playback.

MP4 MKV M3U8 MPD AVI +50 more

IPTV & Live Streaming

Import M3U playlists, add Xtream Codes (channels, movies, series), or paste any stream URL. Full IPTV experience with episode navigation.

Online Subtitles

Search, download and sync subtitles online directly inside the player. Supports SRT, VTT, and custom subtitle styling with color/size controls.

Video Downloader

Paste a compatible playback link and download videos directly to your device for offline viewing anytime.

Cast to Screen

Cast videos to your Chromecast, Smart TV, or any compatible device directly from the player. Keep browsing while you watch on the big screen.

Picture-in-Picture

Continue watching in a floating mini-player while using other apps. Full PiP mode support with Android system integration.

Customizable Controls

Personalize your player bottom bar, pinch-to-zoom, swipe gestures for brightness/volume/seek, playback speed, aspect ratio, and more.

DRM Protected Content

Full Widevine and ClearKey DRM support for protected streams. Pass license URLs and custom auth headers through deep links or intents.

Sleep Timer & More

Built-in sleep timer, screen lock, background audio playback, shuffle, repeat modes, and home screen widgets. Everything you need.
